# FINet Vision AI

## Register

product

## Users

A college student demonstrating camouflaged object segmentation to examiners on a laptop in a normally lit classroom. Examiners need to inspect an image, understand each mask, compare two real models and download evidence.

## Product Purpose

Provide one Gradio interface around the original FINet architecture and its training-only boundary-loss variant, with separate Camouflaged Objects and Factory Quality Inspection model weights. Show genuine inference, measured timings and labelled-data metrics only when the required real inputs exist. Clearly explain missing checkpoints and errors. Factory mode presents candidate defect regions and abstains from product acceptance/rejection without a separately validated policy.

## Brand Personality

Clear, composed, precise. These are implementation choices under the user's request for a professional demonstration, rather than claimed personal styling preferences.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, invented accuracy numbers, simulated segmentation, busy animations and technical training controls in the image-processing workflow.

## Design Principles

- Keep the photograph and segmentation outputs central.
- Label probability, thresholded masks and predicted boundaries distinctly.
- Make model availability and errors actionable.
- Compare models on the identical uploaded image and threshold.
- Preserve original FINet attribution and state actual evidence limits.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labelled native controls, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast and responsive columns. Do not convey model identity or mask meaning through color alone. No decorative motion.
